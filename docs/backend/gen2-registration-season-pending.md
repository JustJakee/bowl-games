# Gen 2 registration and Season Pending staging plan

## Account approval

1. Public visitors create an email/password account in the existing login portal and confirm the emailed Cognito code. Email confirmation leaves the account without a group.
2. A confirmed user can sign in but sees **Awaiting administrator approval** until a fresh Cognito token contains `player` or `admin`.
3. An existing `admin` group member opens **Admin → Approvals**. The admin-only AppSync query lists confirmed, email-verified users without `player` or `admin`; approval rechecks the user in Cognito and adds only the fixed `player` group.
4. The applicant chooses **Check approval** to refresh the Cognito session. Approval does not change `Season.status`, `SeasonConfig`, games, or pick deadlines.

The new `registration-admin` Lambda requires Cognito permissions for `ListUsers`, `AdminGetUser`, `AdminListGroupsForUser`, and `AdminAddUserToGroup`, scoped to the Gen 2 user pool by Amplify Auth `access`. The AppSync operations additionally require the `admin` group. The existing secure-picks Lambda still checks the player group, owner, and server time. Broad season, schedule, profile discovery, and leaderboard entry reads, plus guarded pick operations, now require the `player` group; existing owner-only model reads remain owner-bound and admin model access remains explicit.

**Accepted owner-read exception:** An authenticated account without `player` can still read its own pre-existing `Entry` and `Pick` records through the model owner rules. It cannot read another player's private picks. Direct player `Entry` and `Pick` create/update/delete operations are not granted; entry and pick writes use the player-group-only guarded operations. A pending account can create or update its own `UserProfile` under the existing owner rule, but profile data has no Cognito group-granting path. Only the admin-only approval Lambda calls `AdminAddUserToGroup`. This exception is intentionally limited to owner-held data and must be checked with a pending account in staging.

Before enabling public registration, verify the staging user pool permits self sign-up, requires email-code confirmation, can deliver verification email within its Cognito/SES limits, and has at least one trusted member of the existing `admin` group. Confirm no Cognito trigger or console automation automatically assigns `player` on email confirmation. Do not enable self sign-up manually during this branch push; treat a configuration mismatch as a staging blocker for separate review. Amplify will add the new Lambda, AppSync fields, and Cognito-scoped IAM grants on staging deployment; no manual production pool change is part of this plan.

## Season Pending and historical results

The dashboard displays Season Pending only for the **selected** season while its status is `draft`, or while its status is `open` but the configured `picksOpenAt` is still in the future. The selected season slug continues to come from the existing staging configuration (`VITE_ACTIVE_SEASON_SLUG`); `isTestSeason` is retained. Once an open test season reaches `picksOpenAt`, the normal dashboard, Picks, schedule, and leaderboard appear. The UI derives later Locked, In Progress, and Final presentation from `picksLockAt`, season status, and persisted game statuses. Backend pick locking is unchanged.

The previous 2025 podium, including payouts and tiebreaker details, is preserved as a labeled fallback only when the selected season is in 2026 and no completed 2025 season is available in Gen 2. The suggestion form keeps its Google Forms destination and one-hour local cooldown. For 2026 results in a future season, retain completed 2026 `Season`, `Game`, `Entry`, and `Pick` records in Gen 2 and mark the season complete; the page reads and scores those records. No 2026 winners, payouts, or opening dates are embedded in the frontend. If payouts must display for 2026, add a reviewed historical award source before that season is archived.

## Staging verification before production

1. Review the Amplify change set: new registration Lambda, Cognito-scoped IAM grants, and AppSync operations; verify no existing data table replacement or production pool target.
2. Deploy only `gen2-updates` to the staging Amplify app after approval. Do not run seed or reset commands as part of deployment.
3. Register a new email, verify its code, and confirm it has no Cognito group or broad contest read access. Confirm unverified accounts never appear in Approvals. With a pending account, call the AppSync `createSeasonEntry`, `updateSeasonEntry`, `saveSeasonPick`, and `visibleSeasonPicks` operations directly and verify denial; also verify direct `Entry`/`Pick` writes are denied, another player's private picks remain unreadable, and a direct owner `UserProfile` update cannot change Cognito group membership. If the account owns older Entry/Pick records, verify only those owner reads are allowed.
4. As an admin, approve that user and verify only `player` is added. Confirm a non-admin cannot list or approve registrations, and the applicant gains access only after token refresh. Verify approval and season management remain available to an existing admin before and after season state changes.
5. Select `test26-r2` and verify Pending → Picks Open → Locked → In Progress → Final. During Picks Open, exercise dashboard, Picks, schedule, leaderboard, and a guarded pick save. Admin Approvals and season management must remain available in every state.
6. With a 2026 selected season pending, verify the labeled 2025 historical podium and the suggestion form. Verify the form's cooldown and that completed Gen 2 results take precedence when present.
7. Review CloudWatch errors, AppSync authorization responses, Cognito group membership, and the actual Amplify frontend build. Confirm the deployed approval Lambda's IAM policy has only the four intended Cognito actions on the staging user pool ARN and no wildcard resource. Confirm the CloudFormation change set has no Cognito user pool or DynamoDB table replacement or deletion before applying it. Local synthesis does not prove live IAM, change-set safety, or email delivery.
